02Featured case study

01 · FinanceAI automation + finance + operations

Finance & Portfolio Automation

Turning fragmented financial data and repetitive finance operations into connected, automated workflows.

The problem

Finance teams were spending hours each week manually pulling data from accounting systems into spreadsheets to build recurring reports. Manual financial operations mean moving the same information between accounting software, spreadsheets, email and approval steps, over and over.

The system

An automation pipeline connects accounting APIs to processed data, feeding Google Sheets and a dashboard layer for ongoing financial insight. AI and automation workflows connect the financial systems, process the information, and route actions, with a human review step where a decision matters.

The outcome

6+ finance workflows automated and more than 15 hours of weekly manual work saved for clients.

07About

I enjoy taking messy, repetitive or complex business processes and turning them into systems that actually work.

My work sits between AI engineering, automation, backend systems and business operations. I like understanding how something works today, finding what slows it down, and figuring out how a system can make it simpler and more reliable.

I am particularly interested in applying AI where it creates measurable operational value: a finance team that stops rebuilding the same report by hand, a support queue that gets triaged before anyone opens it, a process that runs on schedule instead of on memory. Less AI for the sake of AI, more systems that quietly do the work.
